Amazon Products Analytics Dashboard

A four-page Power BI dashboard analyzing how pricing, discounts and customer ratings relate across 1,351 Amazon products. It covers customer satisfaction, category performance, product popularity and discount strategy — with a drill-down from category to individual product, slicers for category, rating and price, and a product-level table with conditional formatting. Key finding: deep discounts (50%+) don't lift ratings — most products sit between 4.0 and 4.5 stars whatever the discount.
